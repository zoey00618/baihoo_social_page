01 / OUR COMMITMENTS

The conditions behind
the products matter.

These principles define the workplace we are committed to supporting. Customer assessment should consider actual site conditions and the relevant records.

01

Fair & voluntary employment

We are committed to equal opportunity, respectful treatment, no forced or child labour, and lawful employment conditions covering pay, working time and rest.

02

Health & safety

Our commitment to workplace safety includes attention to hazards, emergency preparedness and the information workers need to do their jobs safely.

03

Worker rights & integrity

We support workers’ rights, including freedom of association in accordance with applicable law, and honest, ethical relationships with customers and partners.

02 / MATERIALS & ENVIRONMENT

Consider the material.
Consider its working life.

01

Recycled material options

Recycled cotton and polyester options are available for selected FR-treated fabrics. Recycled content and supporting documentation are confirmed by article and project.

02

Fit for continued use

Material suitability, durability and care requirements matter alongside recycled content. We consider these factors when discussing fabrics for protective workwear.

03

Resources & production waste

Reducing unnecessary material waste and considering resource use are part of our environmental priorities. We discuss relevant evidence against customers’ sourcing requirements.

Where GRS documentation is applicable, its scope and the selected material must be checked. Recycled content alone does not establish a lower environmental footprint or garment certification.

03 / STANDARDS & ASSESSMENTS

Different frameworks.
Distinct responsibilities.

Our responsible-sourcing discussion includes the programmes below. Each has a different purpose; current certificates, assessment records and their facility scope should be checked as part of supplier review.

SOCIAL PERFORMANCE

amfori BSCI

A social-performance monitoring framework covering labour conditions and responsible business practices. Applicable facility assessment information should be reviewed by scope, date and findings.

SUPPLY-CHAIN TRANSPARENCY

Sedex

Sedex supports responsible-sourcing information exchange. Membership is not certification. Any SMETA report is an audit record, not a certificate or an automatic pass.

PRODUCTION FACILITY

OEKO-TEX STeP

A facility certification covering social responsibility, health and safety, chemical management, environmental performance, environmental management and quality management.

MANAGEMENT SYSTEMS

ISO 9001 / ISO 14001

Quality and environmental management systems support structured management. They are not stand-alone certification of labour conditions or every finished product.

Where product-related marks fit

OEKO-TEX STANDARD 100 addresses harmful-substance testing for the articles covered. RU component recognition and CE conformity relate to applicable components or products. These are separate from social-performance assessments and do not replace a review of working conditions.
