SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY

Respect for people.
Responsibility in production.

We believe protective workwear should be made with care for the people behind it. Our commitment is to safe, fair and respectful working conditions, supported by transparent communication with our customers.

01 / OUR COMMITMENTS

The conditions behind
the products matter.

These principles define the workplace we are committed to supporting. Customer assessment should consider actual site conditions and the relevant records.

01

Respect & equal opportunity

We are committed to fair treatment, equal opportunity and a workplace free from discrimination, harassment and abuse.

02

Voluntary employment

Our commitment is to voluntary employment, no forced labour and no child labour, with lawful protections for young workers where applicable.

03

Working hours & remuneration

Working time, rest periods, remuneration and benefits are priorities within our commitment to lawful employment conditions.

04

Health & safety

A safe working environment requires attention to workplace hazards, emergency preparedness and the information workers need to perform their jobs safely.

05

Worker voice

We support respect for workers’ rights, including freedom of association and collective bargaining in accordance with applicable law.

06

Responsible business conduct

Honest communication and ethical business practices are essential to long-term relationships with workers, customers and supply-chain partners.

02 / STANDARDS & ASSESSMENTS

Different frameworks.
Distinct responsibilities.

Our responsible-sourcing discussion includes the programmes below. Each has a different purpose; current certificates, assessment records and their facility scope should be checked as part of supplier review.

SOCIAL PERFORMANCE

amfori BSCI

A social-performance monitoring framework covering labour conditions and responsible business practices. Applicable facility assessment information should be reviewed by scope, date and findings.

SUPPLY-CHAIN TRANSPARENCY

Sedex

Sedex supports responsible-sourcing information exchange. Membership is not certification. Any SMETA report is an audit record, not a certificate or an automatic pass.

PRODUCTION FACILITY

OEKO-TEX STeP

A facility certification covering social responsibility, health and safety, chemical management, environmental performance, environmental management and quality management.

MANAGEMENT SYSTEMS

ISO 9001 / ISO 14001

Quality and environmental management systems support structured management. They are not stand-alone certification of labour conditions or every finished product.

Where product-related marks fit

OEKO-TEX STANDARD 100 addresses harmful-substance testing for the articles covered. RU component recognition and CE conformity relate to applicable components or products. These are separate from social-performance assessments and do not replace a review of working conditions.

04 / CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT

Responsibility continues
beyond an assessment.

Assessments help identify gaps; lasting progress depends on addressing them. We view customer feedback and applicable review findings as inputs to improvement, rather than treating a single audit as the end of the process.
